# Prototype limitations

- The collection is one US research sample, not a complete market scan. Its size
  is 20 inputs; the number of eligible recommendations is not guaranteed.
- All launch assumptions are hypothetical and shown on the site. Resources of
  the CEO's holding company and state-specific legal requirements are unconfirmed.
- Search is bounded. Unavailable pages, conflicting evidence and missing legal
  or financial context remain visible gaps rather than automatic passes.
- Verifier checks retrieved documents independently of Critic's conclusions.
  This prototype does not provide an unrestricted autonomous search loop for
  every agent. Source agreement is not proof of underlying truth.
- Scores, costs of a business test, timelines and qualitative confidence are
  analytical estimates. They are not calibrated success probabilities.
- The site is a read-only HTTP prototype. Research is started manually on Aeza;
  no owner login, browser launch button, permanent worker, manual-review editor,
  global catalogue or detailed billing dashboard is included.
- Provider usage is calculated from stored tariffs. It is distinct from confirmed
  invoice charges; unknown costs continue to consume their reservation.
- The earlier Calendly result is a real partial assessment with six passing and
  four unresolved criteria. It did not reach Critic/Verifier or final ranking.
  Current collection progress and validation are recorded in
  [implementation notes](docs/implementation.md), not inferred from fixtures.
